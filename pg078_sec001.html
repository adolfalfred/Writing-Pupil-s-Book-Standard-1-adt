Letter W

Trace the uppercase letter W or write dot six, then dots two, four, five, and six.

Copy or finger spell the letter W using the writing lines, keyboard, or braille.

Uppercase W has four long diagonal strokes joined to make two bottom points and a middle peak.

Copy or finger spell uppercase W with lowercase w using the writing lines, keyboard, or braille.

The model shows W followed by w. Uppercase W has four long diagonal strokes joined to make two bottom points and a middle peak. Lowercase w has four diagonal strokes joined to make two low points.
