Lesson 14: Letter R

Lesson 14

Letter R

Trace the letter R in your exercise book.

Accessible response options

Enter R. Use a teacher-provided raised-line model to explore and practise the printed path.

Use a refreshable braille display, braille keyboard, operating-system braille input, or standard keyboard. Six-key translation will be added after the braille code is approved.

This spatial activity also requires a teacher-provided real object, tactile graphic, or raised-line resource. The text response does not replace tactile practice.

Copy uppercase R in your exercise book.

No additional tactile resource is required for this language response.
