Lesson 14: Letter R

Lesson 14

Letter R

Trace the uppercase letter R or write dot six, then dots one, two, three, and five.

Copy or finger spell the letter R using the writing lines, keyboard, or braille.

Uppercase R has a tall vertical stroke, a rounded upper bowl, and a diagonal leg to the lower right.

Copy or finger spell uppercase R with lowercase r using the writing lines, keyboard, or braille.

The model shows R followed by r. Uppercase R has a tall vertical stroke, a rounded upper bowl, and a diagonal leg to the lower right. Lowercase r has a short downstroke with a small rounded shoulder on the right.
