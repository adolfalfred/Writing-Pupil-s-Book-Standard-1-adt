Lesson 20: Letter v

Lesson 20

Letter v

Draw, identify, or describe the pattern for the letter v; explore a teacher-provided raised-line version before answering.

Trace the letter v or write dots one, two, three, and six.

Copy or finger spell the letter v using the writing lines, keyboard, or braille.

Lowercase v has two diagonal strokes that meet at a point on the bottom writing line.
